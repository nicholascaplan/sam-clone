#### **Main Content (Biography)**

* **Opening Statement:**Samantha Fernando (b. 1984\) is a British composer whose work is centred on an exploration of timbre, space, and the unfolding of sound. She is drawn to ideas of landscape, architecture, and place, approaching composition as the act of creating a space in sound, one that an audience can enter and inhabit.  
* **Artistic Philosophy:**In recent years her music has moved towards a language that values clarity, directness, and openness: working with deliberately limited material, allowing music time to breathe, and attending with precision to gesture, texture, and form. Collaboration with performers is central to her practice, particularly in shaping sound worlds that respond to the physicality of instruments and the acoustic space in which music is heard.  
* **Ensembles & Collaborators:**Philharmonia Orchestra, BCMG, Riot Ensemble, The BBC Singers, LOD Muziektheater (Ghent), Silbersee Vocal Ensemble (Amsterdam), The Crossing (USA), and the London Sinfonietta.  
* **Festivals:**Aldeburgh Music, Huddersfield Contemporary Music Festival, Sounds New, Gaudeamus Muziekweek, York Late Music, and the Oxford Lieder Festival.  
* **Key Achievements & Major Works:**  
  * **2013:** Awarded RPS Composition Prize; commissioned for *Breathing Space* (Philharmonia Orchestra / Music of Today series).  
  * **2018:** Commissioned to write *Formations* for the 50th birthday of the London Sinfonietta (premiered at Royal Festival Hall, conducted by Vladimir Jurowski).  
  * **2021:** Finalist for the Arts Foundation Futures Awards.  
  * **2021:** Premiered *Current, Rising*, a hyper-reality opera produced by the Royal Opera House and Figment Productions (directed by Netia Jones, designed by Jo Scotcher).  
  * **2022:** Premiered chamber opera *glass human* (collaboration with Melanie Wilson, directed by Lucy Bailey) at Glyndebourne and UK tour.  
* **Recent Commissions:**  
  * *Balconies* for solo (multi-tracked) violin — written for Olivia de Prato's album *Panorama* (New Focus Recordings, 2023).  
  * *Sound Inhabitants* for large ensemble — commissioned and premiered by London Sinfonietta (2023).  
  * *Wintering* — commissioned by Wigmore Hall, performed by Manchester Collective and the Marian Consort (2025 national tour).  
  * *The Exoplanets* — commissioned by City of London Sinfonia, premiered at Hackney Empire (2025/2026).  
* **Education & Academic Appointments:**  
  * Studied Composition at the Royal Academy of Music and the University of Oxford.  
  * Honorary Research Fellow in Composition at Royal Holloway, University of London.

#### **Project Overview**

* **Commission:** Glyndebourne Tour (Autumn 2022\)  
* **Instrumentation:** Chamber opera for 3 singers, 5 players, and electronics.  
* **Creative Team:**  
  * **Composer:** Samantha Fernando

  * **Designer:** Jo Parker

  * **Grace (Soprano):** Anna Cavaliero  
  * **Oma (Mezzo-Soprano):** Camille Maalawy  
  * **Edward (Baritone):** Denver M. Smith  
  * **Ensemble:** Viola (Daisy Spiers), Cello (Rosie Banks Francis), Harp (Sue Blair), Accordion (Iñigo Mikeleiz Berrade), Percussion (Cameron Sinclair)  
* **Synopsis:**Three lonely people live isolated lives in a tower block: Grace, a geology student away from home for the first time who hears an unsettling voice in her head; Oma, a Syrian refugee waiting for leave to remain; and Edward, bereaved, experiencing the early effects of dementia. A mysterious sound begins to be heard, intensifying their isolation until they are drawn outside to a sinkhole opening in the earth. In these allegorical depths, they face their deepest wounds and find connection with each other.

### **10\. Dedicated Project Page: *Wintering***

* **URL:** https://www.samanthafernando.com/wintering  
* **Page Title:** Wintering — Samantha Fernando

#### **Project Details**

* **Instrumentation:** SATB (4 voices: Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Baritone) \+ String Quartet  
* **Duration:** c. 27 minutes  
* **Commission:** Wigmore Hall  
* **Performers:** Manchester Collective & The Marian Consort  
* **Movement List:**  
  1. *I. Vista*  
  2. *II. Cloud Canvas 1*  
  3. *III. Cloud Canvas 2*  
  4. *IV. To Do: Do Less*  
  5. *V. Snow on Snow*  
  6. *VI. Cocoon*  
* **Program Note:**Inspired by Katherine May's book *Wintering*, exploring the idea of embracing life's fallow periods rather than resisting them. Scored for four voices and string quartet, *Wintering* explores the tension between quiet introspection and the ever-present hum of the outside world, moving through restlessness and retreat toward stillness and quiet resilience.
