# **Comprehensive Website Critique & Content Strategy**

**Target Audience:** Opera companies, ensemble artistic directors, festival curators, orchestral commissioners, grant bodies, and classical music audiences.

**Assumed Context:** Professional artist portfolio and digital press kit for an acclaimed contemporary classical composer.

**Proficiency Level:** Developing.

## **Executive Summary & Growth Areas**

Samantha Fernando’s body of work and high-level commissions (Royal Opera House, Glyndebourne, Wigmore Hall, London Sinfonietta) demonstrate an extraordinary creative voice, offering a remarkably rich foundation for a world-class composer portfolio.

However, in its current state, the website’s content structure and copy suffer from navigation sprawl, passive framing, and unnecessary user friction. Below are the three primary growth areas to address in your rewrite.

### **Growth Area 1: Information Architecture & Navigation Bloat (Structure)**

* **Specific Text Examples:**  
  * Navigation Menu: Home, Events, Past Performances, Works List, Watch, Listen, Writing, Current, Rising, glass human, Wintering, Contact.  
  * Separate media pages (/watch, /music) competing with dedicated project pages (/current-rising, /glasshuman, /wintering) and the main catalog (/workslist).  
* **Diagnosis & Explanation:**  
  * Having 11 top-level navigation items creates severe cognitive overload. Presenters, commissioners, and press typically give a website 10–15 seconds to find what they need.  
  * Media is currently fragmented across five different pages. If a festival programmer wants to evaluate *glass human*, they currently have to check /glasshuman for synopsis, /watch or /music for media, and /workslist for instrumentation specs.  
  * Past Performances overlaps heavily with Events, creating confusion about where to find tour history.  
* **Actionable Next Steps:**  
  * How can we consolidate the 11 navigation links into 4–5 core top-level categories? (e.g., About, Works & Media, Projects, Calendar, Contact).  
  * Consider nesting featured operas (*Current, Rising*, *glass human*) as highlighted case studies inside a unified Works & Media hub rather than giving every work its own top-level menu item.

### **Growth Area 2: Hero Positioning & Bio Copywriting (Messaging & Voice)**

* **Specific Text Examples:**  
  * Main Hero Bio: *"Samantha Fernando (b. 1984\) is a British composer whose work is centred on an exploration of timbre, space, and the unfolding of sound..."*  
  * Philosophy: *"In recent years her music has moved towards a language that values clarity, directness, and openness..."*  
* **Diagnosis & Explanation:**  
  * Leading with (b. 1984\) formats the opening line like a academic dictionary entry or Wikipedia page rather than an engaging hero narrative.  
  * The bio uses standard contemporary classical jargon (*"exploration of timbre, space, and the unfolding of sound"*) which, while accurate, lacks punch and doesn't immediately distinguish her unique position in the field (e.g., her pioneering work in VR/hyper-reality opera and spatial acoustic design).  
  * The bio reads as a passive listing of credentials rather than a compelling narrative of *why* an ensemble or opera house should commission her today.  
* **Actionable Next Steps:**  
  * How might we craft a 2-sentence "elevator pitch" for the homepage hero that pairs her prestige commissions (Royal Opera House, Glyndebourne) directly with her artistic signature (e.g., immersive sound worlds, spatial architecture, human connection)?  
  * Move formal birth years and academic degrees to a downloadable PDF Press Kit/CV section, allowing the web copy to focus on narrative impact.

### **Growth Area 3: Conversion Friction & Call-to-Action (CTA) Hierarchy (User Experience)**

* **Specific Text Examples:**  
  * Listen Page Note: *"More tracks available on my SoundCloud page and Spotify... Please contact me directly if you would like to hear anything that is not available online."*  
  * Works List Catalog: Plain text listing of instrumentation without direct action links for score hire, audio perusal, or technical riders.  
* **Diagnosis & Explanation:**  
  * Contemporary music programmers usually need three things immediately: **Audio snippet**, **Perusal Score (PDF)**, and **Instrumentation / Duration details**.  
  * Asking users to email manually to request audio introduces unnecessary friction. Gatekeepers under tight deadlines will often move on to another composer if audio or score perusal requires an email back-and-forth.  
  * The catalog lists works chronologically/by ensemble size, but lacks clear CTA buttons (e.g., \[Request Perusal Score\], \[Listen\], \[Watch Performance\], \[Book / Inquire\]).  
* **Actionable Next Steps:**  
  * What if every work in the Works List featured a standardized button bar: \[Listen/Watch\], \[Perusal Score\], and \[Inquire for Performance/Hire\]?  
  * Can we set up a password-protected press/perusal locker or direct streaming links so prospective commissioners can listen to private recordings instantly?

## **Proposed Site Sitemap & Architecture Blueprint**

To streamline the user journey, here is a streamlined 4-page site architecture model:

├── 1\. HOME / ABOUT (/about)  
│   ├── Hero Statement (Punchy summary \+ major headline achievements)  
│   ├── Featured Works Carousel (Quick links to Current, Rising / glass human / Wintering)  
│   ├── Full Biography & Editorial Profile  
│   ├── Press Quotes & Praise  
│   └── Education, Awards & Research Appointments  
│  
├── 2\. WORKS & MEDIA (/works)  
│   ├── Filterable Works Catalog (By Genre: Opera, Orchestral, Chamber, Solo, Choral)  
│   ├── Audio & Video Player Hub (Unified streamable media player)  
│   ├── Case Studies (Deep-dives into Current, Rising, glass human, Wintering)  
│   └── Perusal Score & Score Hire Information  
│  
├── 3\. CALENDAR (/calendar)  
│   ├── Upcoming Performances & Premieres  
│   ├── Past Performance Archive  
│   └── Panel & Public Writing / Articles  
│  
└── 4\. CONTACT (/contact)  
    ├── General Inquiries & Score Hire Form  
    ├── Commissioning & Rights Contact  
    └── Downloadable Press Kit (High-res photos \+ official bio PDFs)
